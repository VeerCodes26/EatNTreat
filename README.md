# GoodFurs India • Pet Marketplace & Doorstep Grooming

GoodFurs India is a modern D2C pet marketplace for healthy purebred puppies, dynamic location-based pet grooming (Metro, Town & Village rates), and veterinary care accessories.

## Features
- **Purebred Puppy Adoption Directory**: Verified breeders, KCI certification, health clearances, and real-time breed filters.
- **Dynamic Doorstep Pet Grooming Hub**: Area Tier selector (City, Town, Village) with real-time price multipliers for small, medium, large dogs and cats.
- **Interactive 2026 Price Matrix & EMI Calculator**: Instant 0% downpayment monthly installment calculations.
- **Gamified AI Breed Matcher Quiz**: Step-by-step quiz matching parents with their ideal dog breed.
- **Mobile-First Pet Care Shop**: Touch-optimized cart drawer with 1-click WhatsApp order placement.
- **Authentic GoodFurs Navigation & Sign In Modal**: Direct replication of GoodFurs India brand design tokens and authentication UI.

## Deployment on Vercel
This is a high-performance static website that deploys directly to Vercel with zero build configuration.
